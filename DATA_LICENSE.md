# Data and Code Licensing

This repository distinguishes NEST-authored research artifacts from third-party
source documents and retained source elements.

- **Code:** NEST data-processing, evaluation, and analysis code is released under
  the MIT License.
- **NEST-authored artifacts:** NEST annotations, schemas, metadata, prompts,
  synthetic contexts and filled instances, and derived evaluation metadata are
  released under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt) unless a file states
  otherwise.
- **Original third-party documents:** Original PDFs, source images, and Office
  documents are not distributed by NEST.
- **Normalized benchmark inputs:** Normalized HTML and HTML-rendered PNG inputs are
  distributed with provenance and source-terms notices for benchmark use and are
  not relicensed as NEST-authored content where they retain third-party elements.

The original 828-record manifest and a current 869-record index are provided
for transparency. A manifest entry documents the best available source
information; it does not itself grant or replace rights under the applicable
source terms.

Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses.

The current text-task inputs, annotations, metadata, and synthetic artifacts are
distributed together. Rendered PNGs cover all 869 records; Stages 7-8 cover only
the original 828. Original third-party PDF, image, and Office files are not
benchmark inputs and are not distributed.

Versioned batches under [`extensions/`](extensions/README.md) extend the combined
text-task inventory to 1,500 templates. Each batch includes source attribution,
reuse terms, and collection records alongside its task artifacts. Applicable
source terms continue to govern retained third-party elements; the inventory
total does not imply a single license for every source form.
