# Data statement

This extension contains ten normalized blank administrative templates and synthetic completion examples. It contains no actual applicant records, no source logos or artwork, and no original DOCX, PDF, or ODT files. The issuing agencies, original URLs, reuse statements, and original hashes are recorded in [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md) and [manifest.json](manifest.json).

The forms are research transcriptions, not filing instructions or official current application channels. Several source documents bear historical dates or regulation references. Attribution and source links should accompany redistribution. No issuing agency endorses the extension.

No model baseline scores are provided.
