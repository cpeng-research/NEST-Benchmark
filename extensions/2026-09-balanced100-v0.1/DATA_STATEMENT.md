# Data Statement

Sampling prioritizes Education and Healthcare, and French and Spanish, to improve the current catalog's sparse groups. This is not a representative survey. The six existing domain labels are unchanged; business topics are secondary metadata.

Complete forms are represented in normalized semantic layouts rather than pixel-identical facsimiles. Instructions, conditions, fields, choices and repeated rows are recorded in the source annotations. Source editions may be historical.

Filled values and contexts are synthetic test cases, not real personal records or valid signatures. No original third-party documents, logos, artwork or fonts are distributed.

Retained source material follows the reuse conditions listed in SOURCE.md. NEST-authored annotations and synthetic test materials follow the repository's applicable data licence.
