Part A – Selection
Reason for LLP cancellation (tick only one box):

The LLP student was not enrolled as a full-time student in a qualifying educational program when you made the LLP withdrawal but had received written notification that they were entitled to enrol before March of the following year and did not enrol in time.

You became a non-resident of Canada before the end of the year in which you made the LLP withdrawal.

The LLP student left the program before April of the year after the LLP withdrawal, and 75% or more of the student's tuition was refundable.
