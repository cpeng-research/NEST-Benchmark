TACHOGRAPHS ON PUBLIC SERVICE VEHICLES
This form is to be completed and submitted at time of test if a vehicle does not have a calibrated tachograph fitted according to the requirements of IM26 of the Public Service Vehicle Inspection Manual and the presenter wishes to declare it to be exempt. One of the following reasons should be selected :
• Vehicle was manufactured prior to 1 January 1947.
• Vehicle is over 25 years old and is not used commercially for carriage of passengers.
• Vehicle propelled by steam.
• Vehicle is not capable by design of exceeding 40km/h under its own power on level road.
• Vehicle is only used on regular scheduled services with a route length not exceeding 50 km (also includes former stage carriage type buses presented by a vehicle dealer).
• Vehicle is used by or on behalf of a relevant NHS body or other authorised body to provide ambulance services or to transport medical personnel.
• Vehicle is used by a local authority for relevant social services functions.
• Vehicle is used by an airport authority within airport perimeters or used on roads inside hub facilities such as a port, inter-port or railway terminal.
• Vehicle is used by or under the control of the armed services, civil defence, fire services or forces responsible for maintaining public order.
• Vehicle is used in emergencies or rescue operations (including humanitarian aid).
• Vehicle is used for driving instruction and examination with a view to obtaining a driving licence or certificate of professional competence and is not used for the commercial carriage of goods or passengers.
• Vehicle is used on an island not exceeding 2300 square kilometres in area not linked to the rest of Great Britain by a bridge, tunnel or ford open to motor vehicles.
