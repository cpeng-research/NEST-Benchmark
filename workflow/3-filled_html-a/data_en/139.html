*4. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-05-10 at approximately 10:30, workers from XYZ Civil Engineering Co. carried out excavation works at the site adjacent to the utility verge at 123 Example Street without providing prior notice to the FNO and without installing temporary protection or safe digging measures around the clearly marked underground telecommunications line. During excavation, the worker operating the backhoe struck and displaced protective conduit around the UTL. No trial holes or hand-digging to expose the UTL were performed at the point of impact. The activity appears to be a suspected contravention of section 18A of the TO regarding requirements for work near UTLs. I am lodging this complaint to request an investigation and enforcement action where appropriate.
