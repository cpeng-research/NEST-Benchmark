5. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-04-18 a SmartCom salesperson visited the subject party and offered a promotional mobile plan described as "unlimited data" with no additional fees. The contract provided at sign-up included a hidden monthly surcharge and an early termination fee not disclosed by the salesperson. The salesperson verbally guaranteed free international roaming, which was not available. Billing statements subsequently included charges inconsistent with the salesperson's representations. The complainant believes these omissions and misleading statements contravene the fair trading provisions of the TDO.
