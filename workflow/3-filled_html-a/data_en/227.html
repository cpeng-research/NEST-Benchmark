5. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-04-15 I visited ABC Telecom retail outlet and was offered a promotional plan advertised as a 'free handset with 12-month contract'. The salesperson verbally confirmed a 12-month commitment and no upfront fees. After signing the contract on 2026-04-15 I was charged a $200 activation fee and placed on a 24-month contract in the written agreement. The monthly fee shown in the advertisement ($98) does not match the $128 monthly fee on the contract. I attempted to resolve the matter with the store and customer service; they refused to amend the contract or refund the activation fee. I believe ABC Telecom misrepresented the offer and failed to disclose key contract terms, potentially breaching the fair trading sections of the TDO.
