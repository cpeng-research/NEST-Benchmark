Summary of circumstances:
1) Facility damage and repairs: On January 15, 2026, the charity's primary warehouse and distribution centre sustained significant roof and structural damage following an ice-dam event. Emergency temporary repairs and safety remediation required closure of the distribution centre for six weeks (January 16 to February 28, 2026). The total estimated repair cost is $60,000. Insurance coverage applies but there is a $10,000 deductible and an anticipated claims processing delay of 4–6 months; interim cash requirements have forced the charity to reallocate funds previously designated for qualifying program disbursements.

2) Temporary program suspension and reduced in-kind receipts: The closure resulted in suspension of two regular community meal programs and a large volunteer-run food distribution for four weeks, reducing qualifying disbursements (direct food distribution and program delivery) by an estimated $8,500 for the fiscal year.

3) Reduced donations and fundraising revenue: A local economic downturn and the timing of the closure coinciding with our major year-end campaign produced a 22% reduction in monetary donations relative to projections for the period November 2025–March 2026. This represents an estimated revenue shortfall of $12,000, part of which would normally have been directed to program disbursements during the fiscal year.

Net impact and reduction sought:
- Expected increase in non-program expenditures (repairs, temporary storage, insurance deductible): $10,000 (out-of-pocket) to be paid in the 2026 fiscal year.
- Estimated lost qualifying disbursements due to program suspension and reduced donations: $8,500.
- Total net shortfall against planned qualifying disbursements: approximately $18,500. After applying available reserve reallocation and minor expense cuts ($3,500), the charity requires a reduction of $15,000 to the Disbursement Quota for the 2026 fiscal year to avoid depleting operating reserves and ensure continuity of essential services.

Supporting documents attached (submitted with this application):
- Detailed repair invoices and contractor estimates (dated Jan–Mar 2026).
- Insurance claim submission and correspondence indicating deductible and processing timeline.
- Monthly income and donation reports November 2025–March 2026 showing the shortfall.
- Calculation worksheet summarizing projected qualifying disbursements before and after the incident.

The requested reduction is temporary for the specified taxation year and is required to allow the charity to complete necessary repairs, restore program capacity, and maintain solvency while awaiting insurance recovery and the return to normal donation levels.
