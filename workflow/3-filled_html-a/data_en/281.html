I contracted with Acme Contracting Ltd. during 2024 to perform specialized installation work under fixed-term contracts and invoiced for completed projects. Acme treated me as an independent contractor and did not withhold CPP contributions. The ruling concluded I was self-employed and not entitled to CPP coverage; I disagree because Acme exercised control over my schedule, provided job-specific training and supplied all materials and tools, and I was economically dependent on Acme for ongoing work. I therefore appeal the classification and the amount of insurable earnings assessed.
