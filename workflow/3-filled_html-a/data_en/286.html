Issue: Reassessment under the Select Luxury Items Tax Act (SLITA) for motor vehicle purchase reported for the 2023 taxation period. Relief sought: Cancellation of the reassessment or reduction to reflect the correct taxable amount, issuance of refund for any amounts paid in respect of the reassessment plus statutory interest, and confirmation that no further penalty applies.
Facts and reasons: On 2023-06-15 I purchased a motor vehicle (VIN: 1HGBH41JXMN109186) for personal use. The seller's invoice and supporting documentation show a purchase price of CAD 48,200. The Canada Revenue Agency reassessed on 2024-03-15 asserting a taxable value of CAD 55,000 and applied SLITA tax accordingly. The reassessment is incorrect because the taxable value was derived from an inflated valuation that does not reflect the actual consideration paid or the fair market value at the time of purchase. Evidence supporting my position includes the original purchase invoice, bank transfer records, the seller's bill of sale, an independent third‑party appraisal dated 2023-06-20 and photos of the vehicle at sale showing condition consistent with the stated price. The reassessment did not adequately consider the documented deductions for prior damage and agreed price adjustments reflected on the bill of sale. I request that the reassessment be vacated or corrected to the documented purchase price, that any tax already paid above the correct amount be refunded with interest, and that no penalties be imposed given there was no intent to underreport. I rely on the invoice, payment records, appraisal, and correspondence with the seller as the basis for these facts and requests.
