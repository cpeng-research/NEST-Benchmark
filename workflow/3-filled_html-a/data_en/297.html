2. Explain the duties and responsibilities of management and key staff.
Alice M. Thompson (CEO): Provides overall strategic leadership, sets long-term company vision, leads the executive team, chairs the Board of Directors meetings, approves major investments and partnerships, and represents the company to stakeholders and investors. Responsible for corporate governance, performance targets, and final decision-making on material matters.

Benjamin K. Rivera (CFO): Manages all financial functions including budgeting, financial planning and analysis, accounting, treasury and tax. Prepares financial reports, ensures regulatory and tax compliance, oversees capital structure and funding activities, and advises the CEO and Board on financial risks and opportunities.

Caroline S. Ng (CTO): Leads product and technology strategy, oversees research & development, software engineering, IT infrastructure and information security. Responsible for product roadmap, technical architecture, delivery timelines, quality assurance, and integration of new technologies to maintain competitive advantage.

David O. Martin (COO): Oversees day-to-day operations, supply chain, logistics, and operational efficiency. Implements operational policies, manages cross-functional execution of projects, ensures service delivery standards, and coordinates with other executives to translate strategy into operational plans and KPIs.

Elena V. Petrova (Head of Sales & Marketing): Develops and executes go-to-market strategy, brand positioning, demand generation, sales planning and customer relationship management. Responsible for revenue growth, channel management, pricing strategy, and marketing communications to acquire and retain customers.

Farid H. Khan (Head of Human Resources & Compliance): Manages human capital strategy, recruitment, performance management, compensation and benefits, training and development, and employee relations. Oversees regulatory compliance, internal policies, risk management related to HR, and coordinates internal audits and compliance reporting.

Collective responsibilities: The executive team meets regularly to review performance against strategic objectives, set annual plans and budgets, assess risks, and make decisions on major capital expenditures, hiring of senior personnel, and significant commercial contracts. Each executive provides monthly reports to the Board and coordinates cross-functional leadership to ensure alignment on company priorities, regulatory compliance, financial controls, and continuous improvement of operational processes.
