Description of Trainee/Intern's role for this program or phase
The trainee will participate in hands-on laboratory research focused on characterization of gene expression pathways and protein interaction networks. Responsibilities include performing molecular cloning, PCR/qPCR, Western blotting, immunoprecipitation assays, cell culture maintenance, preparation of reagents and buffers, data collection, data entry into laboratory information management systems, routine equipment calibration, and contributing to weekly lab meetings and progress reports. The trainee will also assist in troubleshooting experiments and maintaining laboratory notebooks in accordance with institutional standards.
Specific goals and objectives for this program or phase
1) Acquire proficiency in core molecular biology techniques (cloning, PCR/qPCR, Western blot, immunoprecipitation) and demonstrate accurate execution of at least five representative protocols by week 12. 2) Design and execute a small independent sub-project under supervision, including hypothesis generation, experimental planning, data collection, and preliminary analysis, resulting in a written interim report by week 20. 3) Develop competence in laboratory data management and interpretation, including use of LIMS and basic statistical analysis tools, demonstrated by proper documentation and presentation at the end-of-phase seminar. 4) Improve scientific communication skills through participation in weekly lab meetings and by delivering one oral presentation and one written summary of findings by phase end.
