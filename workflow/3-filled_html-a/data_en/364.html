Section 4 – Circumstances and facts supporting your request
I am requesting a waiver of the Part X.1 tax on excess RRSP contributions for 2021 and 2022 due to a series of inadvertent errors and mitigating circumstances. In late 2020 my employer's payroll provider incorrectly reported a pension transfer, which resulted in duplicate contributions being processed in 2021; I only became aware of the excess after receiving year-end statements and immediately arranged to withdraw the excess amounts and applied the required forms. Additionally, during 2021 I experienced a prolonged medical issue that limited my ability to manage financial matters and communicate with the plan administrator promptly. I have not benefited from the excess contributions beyond their return, have cooperated fully with plan administrators to correct the amounts, and the financial hardship associated with the assessed tax would be disproportionate given the unintentional nature of the error. I respectfully request that the CRA waive the Part X.1 tax on the grounds of circumstances of undue hardship and reasonable cause, and I am prepared to provide supporting documentation (medical records, correspondence with the employer and plan administrator, and transaction records) upon request.
