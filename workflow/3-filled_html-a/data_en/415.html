Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP) record sheet
About this form
More help and information
This form is for employers to use to record details of their employee’s Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP). You must keep these details. You do not have to use this form, but you may find it helpful. However, you must keep SPP records for at least 3 years after the end of the tax year to which they relate.
For more information on how and when you must pay SPP and what records you must keep, go to www.gov.uk/employers-paternity-pay-leave/records. If you need help with this form or with the SPP scheme, you can contact the Employer Helpline on 0300 200 3200.
How to use this SPP record sheet
Please read these notes and fill in the form, making any additional notes on page 2 if appropriate. This record sheet is in 2 parts. Fill in: • Record of notification of paternity absence when your employee tells you the date they want to start their paternity absence. Your employee will give you form SC3 ‘Becoming a parent’, form SC4 `Becoming an adoptive parent or parental order parent’ or form SC5 ‘`SPP and paternity leave when adopting from abroad’ • Record of SPP period when their pay period is about to start. You must keep a record of this period even if your employee cannot get SPP in one or both of the weeks or does not come back to work afterwards
