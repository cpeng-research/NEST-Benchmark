a. Identified Risk/Concern
Risk of medication mismanagement (missed doses, incorrect dosing, or taking contraindicated medications) which could lead to adverse reactions, unstable medical conditions, or hospitalization. Participant prefers to self-manage medications despite the risk.
b. Details of the Participant's Plan to Address the Risk/Concern
Participant will self-administer medications using a weekly pill organizer and a printed medication schedule. Participant agrees to have medications pre-packaged by the pharmacy when available and to bring all medications to monthly medication reconciliation with the SC RN Consultant. Participant consents to a twice-weekly check-in call from the Care Coordinator for the first three months, then weekly telephonic check-ins thereafter if clinically indicated. Emergency contact (family member) will be notified if participant is unreachable for two consecutive check-ins. Participant will keep a written log of any missed doses and report any side effects immediately to the SC RN Consultant or primary care physician.
c. Possible Consequences if the Risk/Concern is Not Addressed
Possible consequences include medication errors resulting in adverse drug events, worsening of chronic conditions, increased risk of falls or cognitive decline, emergency department visits, hospitalization, long-term loss of independence, and in severe cases, death.
