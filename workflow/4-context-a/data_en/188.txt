Background: This document outlines the instructions, required documentation, and data elements needed to request and process an honorarium payment, including steps for current employees, deadlines, and the distinct requirements for new domestic and international recipients, as well as departmental and Accounts Payable responsibilities.

Paying Department:

Important processing notes: 1) If the recipient is a current employee of GW, or has been an employee at any point during the current calendar year, process the request through Payroll. 2) The form needs to be filled out completely and in advance of the event. 3) For a new domestic honorarium recipient, submit the completed and approved Honorarium Recipient Form to Accounts Payable using GW iBuy+ Invoice; a new honorarium recipient is required to attach a completed and signed W-9 form to the Honorarium Recipient Form. Following review by Accounts Payable, the honorarium form with the W-9 form will be forwarded to Supplier Maintenance for registration. 4) For a new international recipient, send the completed and approved Honorarium Recipient Form to Accounts Payable using GW iBuy+ Invoice; a W-8BEN and an Alien Information Request Form are required to be attached to the Honorarium Recipient Form. See more information about rules and conditions for payment, including payment guidelines, tax treatment, and special rules relating to payment of honoraria to individuals who are not U.S. citizens or permanent residents.
