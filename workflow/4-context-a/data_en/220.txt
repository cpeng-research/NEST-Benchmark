Part I: To Licensing Authority, Venue Safety Department. This application concerns the use of laser effects at the venue Esplanade - Theatres on the Bay, Concert Hall for the event Summer Music Festival - Evening Concert Series. Performance dates are 15 July 2026 - 17 July 2026, occurring on days 15, 16, 17 in the month of July in the year 2026, with a total number of performances of 3.

Opening Act - Band A is scheduled from 20:00 to 20:05 (5 minutes). The laser product to be used is a Laserworld CS-2000RGB. The planned display is a stage wash and aerial beam patterns above the stage, with no audience scanning. Safety measures: beams will be confined to the stage and overhead; interlocks and key switch enabled; the operator will perform a pre-show safety sweep; audience scanning is disabled; beam paths will be verified for safe divergence and elevation.

Mid Set - DJ Performance is scheduled from 21:00 to 21:10 (10 minutes). The laser product to be used is an Elation KL Panel Q2. The planned display is graphic beam effects into the upper auditorium and stage backdrops, with low-angle silhouette beams on stage only. Safety measures: ILDA control limits will be applied; attenuation will be used for any beams angled toward seating; a trained operator will be onsite; visible beam path checks will be conducted and an operator emergency kill switch will be positioned front-of-house.

Finale - Full Cast is scheduled from 22:30 to 22:35 (5 minutes). The laser system will be a Pangolin FB4 controller with KVANT ClubMAX 3000. The planned display is fast-moving aerial beams and strobes synchronized to music, limited to overhead and stage effects only. Safety measures: a dedicated safety officer with a kill switch will be at FOH; there will be no direct eye-level audience scanning; all reflective surfaces will be masked; the operator is certified for Class 4 operation; pre-show divergence and irradiance measurements will be logged.

Note: 1. Do not look directly to the origin of laser beam. 2. No laser beam or its reflection shall be directed to persons (including audience, performers and operators liable to view the beam or its reflection).
