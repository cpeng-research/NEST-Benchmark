Background: This application concerns the use of temporary electrical appliances on stage at the Grand Theatre, Hong Kong Cultural Centre for a theatrical performance. The proposal covers temporary stage lighting, a smoke/haze machine, and LED signage to be used during the Summer Gala - Charity Concert scheduled across multiple performances in July 2026.

Part I: To Venue Management, Hong Kong Cultural Centre. Venue: Grand Theatre, Hong Kong Cultural Centre.

Detail: Use of temporary stage lighting, smoke/haze machine and LED signage for theatrical performance.

Performance Date(s): 2026-07-10 to 2026-07-12. Day(s): Friday, Saturday, Sunday. Mth(s): July. Yr(s):. No. of Perf.: 3. Event: Summer Gala - Charity Concert.

Act 1: Opening — Action Area: Main Stage (Full stage wash). Temporary Electrical Appliance Brand & Model: Chauvet DJ Intimidator Spot 260 (230V). Description of Action and any particular safety measures: 19:00 - 19:15 (15 minutes); moving head spotlight and colour wash across stage. Installed by registered electrician; WR1 form applied; circuits protected by RCD; cables routed in cable ramps and securely taped; on-site technician during operation; visual inspection before each performance. Attachment/Diagram: Lighting plot L1; WR1 copy on file.

Act 2: Dance Sequence — Action Area: Main Stage (Center & Wings). Temporary Electrical Appliance Brand & Model: Antari M-7S Smoke Machine (230V). Description of Action and any particular safety measures: 19:20 - 19:35 (15 minutes); low-lying smoke/haze to enhance lighting beams. Unit PAT tested and serviced; uses water-based fluid only; operated by certified technician; local ventilation plan in place between scenes; fire marshal informed; smoke detector masking protocol approved by venue. Attachment/Diagram: Haze plan H2; MSDS attached.

Act 3: Finale — Action Area: Main Stage (Upstage truss). Temporary Electrical Appliance Brand & Model: Elation Strobe LED Pro (230V). Description of Action and any particular safety measures: 21:00 - 21:10 (10 minutes); high-intensity strobe effect during finale. Audience warned of strobe effects in programme and announcements; fixtures securely clamped with secondary safety cables; RCD protection on supply; operator standby; timers set to limit duration. Attachment/Diagram: Cue sheet C3; Warning notice sample.

Prop: Electric Portable Sign — Action Area: Downstage Left (prop riser). Temporary Electrical Appliance Brand & Model: BrightLED S12 Custom LED Strip (12V DC) with isolated PSU. Description of Action and any particular safety measures: Installed before curtain; powered throughout performance; illuminated stage signage and band name display. Low-voltage isolated PSU used; wiring enclosed in trunking; fuses and RCD on supply; secured mounting to prevent movement; inspected and certified by registered electrician. Attachment/Diagram: Prop wiring diagram P4.
