Background/context: This submission concerns a complaint against a telecommunications/broadcasting licensee alleging suspected contravention of the "fair trading sections" of the Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362). The complainant reported alleged misleading advertising, failure to disclose contract terms, unexpected charges, and incorrect representation of contract duration. The telecommunications licensee named in the complaint is ABC Telecom Limited.

The complainant has lodged the matter with another enforcement agency. The complaint was reported to the Customs and Excise Department on 2026-05-10, reference number CEX/2026/12345. The Department acknowledged receipt and opened a preliminary inquiry, requested supporting documents, and indicated further follow-up pending review.

Details of the complaint: On 2026-04-15 the complainant visited an ABC Telecom retail outlet and was offered a promotional plan advertised as a "free handset with 12-month contract." The salesperson verbally confirmed a 12-month commitment and no upfront fees. After signing the contract on 2026-04-15, the complainant was charged a $200 activation fee and found that the written agreement placed them on a 24-month contract. The monthly fee shown in the advertisement ($98) does not match the $128 monthly fee on the contract. The complainant attempted to resolve the matter with the store and with customer service; they refused to amend the contract or refund the activation fee. The complainant believes ABC Telecom misrepresented the offer and failed to disclose key contract terms, potentially breaching the fair trading sections of the TDO.

Records of correspondence: Yes. f. Identification documents of the salespersons involved: No. g. Recordings of conversations with the salespersons involved: Yes. h.
