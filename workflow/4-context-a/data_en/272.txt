Background: The following is a medication list that specifies each prescription medication along with the purpose or reason it is taken, the dose, the time(s) of day it is taken, the form, and any special instructions to follow.

Lisinopril: Purpose or Reason Taken — Hypertension (blood pressure control). Dose — 20 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (morning). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — Take with a full glass of water; avoid potassium supplements and potassium-rich salt substitutes.

Metformin XR: Purpose or Reason Taken — Type 2 diabetes (glycemic control). Dose — 1000 mg. Time(s) of Day — Twice daily (with breakfast and dinner). Form — extended-release tablet. Special Instructions — Take with food to reduce gastrointestinal upset; do not crush or chew.

Atorvastatin: Purpose or Reason Taken — Hyperlipidemia (lower LDL cholesterol). Dose — 40 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (evening). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — Take at bedtime; avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice.

Levothyroxine: Purpose or Reason Taken — Hypothyroidism (thyroid hormone replacement). Dose — 75 mcg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (morning, 30-60 minutes before breakfast). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — Take on an empty stomach 30-60 minutes before eating; separate from calcium/iron by 4 hours.

Sertraline: Purpose or Reason Taken — Major depressive disorder / anxiety. Dose — 50 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (morning). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — May cause drowsiness; avoid alcohol; monitor for mood changes or suicidal thoughts.

Albuterol (salbutamol) inhaler: Purpose or Reason Taken — Bronchospasm relief (asthma/COPD rescue). Dose — 2 puffs per use. Time(s) of Day — As needed (every 4-6 hours PRN). Form — inhaler. Special Instructions — Shake well before use; prime inhaler before first use; rinse mouth after use if steroid inhaler is used concurrently.

Omeprazole: Purpose or Reason Taken — Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD). Dose — 20 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (morning). Form — capsule. Special Instructions — Take 30-60 minutes before a meal; do not chew or crush delayed-release capsules.

Gabapentin: Purpose or Reason Taken — Neuropathic pain. Dose — 300 mg. Time(s) of Day — Three times daily (morning, afternoon, bedtime). Form — capsule. Special Instructions — May cause dizziness or drowsiness; avoid driving until you know how it affects you.

Warfarin: Purpose or Reason Taken — Anticoagulation (stroke prevention / DVT prophylaxis). Dose — 5 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (evening). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — Monitor INR regularly; maintain consistent vitamin K intake; report unusual bleeding or bruising.

Hydrochlorothiazide: Purpose or Reason Taken — Hypertension (diuretic). Dose — 25 mg. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (morning). Form — tablet. Special Instructions — Take in the morning to avoid nocturia; monitor electrolytes and renal function.

Insulin glargine: Purpose or Reason Taken — Basal insulin for diabetes mellitus. Dose — 20 units. Time(s) of Day — Once daily (bedtime). Form — injection. Special Instructions — Rotate injection sites; store unopened vials in refrigerator; adjust dose based on blood glucose monitoring.
