We request a reduction of $15,000 ($15000.00) for the 2026 fiscal year; attached calculations explain the amount specified and show the projected shortfall in available resources for qualifying expenditures due to unforeseen capital repairs and a drop in donations.

This represents an estimated revenue shortfall of $12,000, part of which would normally have been directed to program disbursements during the fiscal year.

Net impact and reduction sought: expected increases in non-program expenditures for repairs, temporary storage, and the insurance deductible amount to $10,000 (out-of-pocket) to be paid in the 2026 fiscal year. Estimated lost qualifying disbursements due to program suspension and reduced donations total $8,500, so the total net shortfall against planned qualifying disbursements is approximately $18,500. After applying available reserve reallocation and minor expense cuts totaling $3,500, the charity requires a reduction of $15,000 to the Disbursement Quota for the 2026 fiscal year to avoid depleting operating reserves and ensure continuity of essential services.
