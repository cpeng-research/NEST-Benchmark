Background/context: LOGO COMPANY is Acme Tech Solutions, located at 123 Innovation Drive, Suite 400, Techville, CA 94000, and the company website is www.acmetech.com. This document describes the process for New Employee Onboarding at Acme Tech Solutions to ensure consistent, compliant, and positive experiences for new hires across the organization.

General information: Process Title: New Employee Onboarding. Department: Human Resources. Contact Info: HR Department - hr@acmetech.com | +1 (555) 123-4567. SOP ID: HR-SOP-001. Effective Date: 2026-05-17. Revision Number: 1.0.

Process overview: Process Description: This procedure defines the standardized process for onboarding new employees, covering pre-start activities, first-day orientation, system and facility provisioning, training assignments, and milestone reviews through confirmation of employment. Purpose & Scope: Purpose: Ensure a consistent, compliant, and positive onboarding experience for all new hires. Scope: Applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract hires across all Acme Tech Solutions locations. Covers HR, IT, Facilities, Payroll, Hiring Managers, and Training teams.

Definitions and related documents: Definitions: 'New Hire' - individual with accepted employment offer; 'HRIS' - Human Resources Information System; 'Onboarding Checklist' - standardized list of tasks to complete for each new hire. Related Documents: Offer Letter Template, Employee Handbook, Background Check Policy, I-9 and Tax Forms, HRIS User Guide, IT Account Provisioning Guide, Facilities Access Request Form.

Process steps (WBS, Task, Owner): WBS 1.0 — Receive signed offer and confirm start date. Owner: HR Coordinator. WBS 1.1 — Create employee record in HRIS and assign employee ID. Owner: HRIS Administrator. WBS 1.2 — Send welcome email with new hire packet, required forms, and first-day agenda. Owner: HR Coordinator. WBS 1.3 — Prepare and provide employment agreement and required new hire paperwork (I-9, W-4, direct deposit). Owner: HR Manager. WBS 1.4 — Initiate background check, education and employment verification. Owner: Talent Acquisition Specialist. WBS 1.5 — Set up payroll profile and enroll employee in benefits per eligibility. Owner: Payroll Specialist. WBS 1.6 — Request IT account creation (email, network access, collaboration tools). Owner: IT Service Desk. WBS 1.7 — Allocate hardware and software licenses, image devices, and prepare peripherals. Owner: IT Asset Manager. WBS 1.8 — Prepare workspace, badge/access card, parking and security access. Owner: Facilities Manager. WBS 1.9 — Schedule orientation sessions and assign orientation materials. Owner: Training Coordinator. WBS 2.0 — Conduct first-day orientation (company overview, policies, benefits overview). Owner: HR Generalist. WBS 2.1 — Hiring manager meets with new hire to review role, expectations and team introductions. Owner: Hiring Manager. WBS 2.2 — Assign job-specific training plan and required learning modules. Owner: Training Coordinator. WBS 2.3 — Complete mandatory compliance, security and data privacy training. Owner: Security Officer. WBS 2.4 — Set up initial performance goals and 30/60/90 day milestones. Owner: Hiring Manager. WBS 2.5 — Conduct 30-day check-in to review progress, address issues and update training plan. Owner: HR Business Partner. WBS 2.6 — Conduct 60-day development review and adjust goals or support as needed. Owner: Hiring Manager & HR Business Partner. WBS 2.7 — Conduct 90-day probationary review and confirm employment status or recommend actions. Owner: HR Manager.
