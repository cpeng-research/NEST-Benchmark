6. Detail the reasons for requesting EMT Selective Certification (Use additional sheets if necessary): The department is requesting selective certification due to a sustained shortage of certified EMTs at the required levels. Recent retirements and increased call volume have strained coverage, resulting in unfilled vacancies and reliance on overtime and mutual aid. Recruitment has been challenged by regional competition and the need for specialized training for ALS care. Selective certification will allow targeted hiring to restore minimum staffing levels, maintain ALS-capable response on all shifts, and improve response reliability while the department implements longer-term recruitment and retention strategies.
