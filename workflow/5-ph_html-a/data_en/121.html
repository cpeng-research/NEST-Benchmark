Form 433-A (OIC) (April 2026)
Collection Information Statement for Wage Earners and Self-Employed Individuals
Use this form if you are
  • An individual who owes income tax on a Form 1040, U.S. Individual Income Tax Return
  • An individual with a personal liability for Excise Tax
  • An individual responsible for a Trust Fund Recovery Penalty
  • An individual who is self-employed or has self-employment income.
  • An individual who is personally responsible for a partnership liability
  • An individual who is submitting an offer on behalf of the estate of a deceased person
Note: Include attachments if additional space is needed... This form should only be used with the Form 656, Offer in Compromise.
