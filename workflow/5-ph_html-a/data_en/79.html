Prerequisite Medical Requirements for EMT Students
Welcome and thank you for your interest in the UCLA Center for Prehospital Care EMT Program! This information is provided to help you meet the documentation and medical requirements for the EMT program. These requirements are developed by the Department of Health and Human Services and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. In addition to needing the below medical requirements for your EMT program, our clinical/field affiliates will require the same immunizations in order for you to participate in their program.
Medical Services
Your physician's office should be able to provide everything you need to complete your EMT program prerequisites. Physician's offices and pharmacies (such as CVS) that have walk up services may also be able to complete the health requirements. For other locations in Los Angeles County please visit the Department of Health Services web site at: www.ladhs.org/clinics/.

The following items are required to complete the ride along portion of the EMT course. The ride alongs must be completed within 30 days of the end of the course so it is important to complete this documentation as soon as possible. When complete bring this form with you and turn it in to the program coordinator. Please clearly denote the required vaccinations if you are including multiple health forms.
Student Requirements Coordinator Initials (for office use)
1. Copy of a current American Heart Association or American Red Cross Basic Life Support for Healthcare Provider card (signed-copy front and back of card). [CoordinatorInitials]
2. Proof of health insurance coverage including private insurer, or state provided insurance, or federal provided insurance (copy front and back of card). [CoordinatorInitials]
3. Proof of Hepatitis B Vaccine or refusal as demonstrated on the Hepatitis Verification Form. [CoordinatorInitials]
4. Completion of the Flu Vaccine Requirement form. [CoordinatorInitials]
5. Proof of current Measles/Mumps/Rubella (MMR) immunization or titer. [CoordinatorInitials]
6. Proof of current Tetanus/Diphtheria/Pertussis (Tdap) immunization or titer. [CoordinatorInitials]
7. Proof of current varicella (chicken pox or VZV) immunization, or titer, or a signed and dated note from a physician indicating you have had the chicken pox. [CoordinatorInitials]
8. Proof of negative TB skin test (within 3 months), or Quantiferon-Gold blood test (within 3 months), or a negative chest x-ray (within 2 years). [CoordinatorInitials]
9. Original Physical and Mask Fit Clearance form, found in this packet, stamped or signed and dated from a physician within 6 months prior to the start of class specifying that you can participate in the clinical portion of the EMT program without physical limitations. [CoordinatorInitials]
10. Proof of COVID19 Vaccination. While the vaccine may not be required for your specific course, if you have received vaccination, please include a copy. [CoordinatorInitials]
