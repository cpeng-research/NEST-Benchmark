*4. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-04-27 at approximately 14:15, workers from Brightway Utilities Services commenced trenching works along the footpath adjacent to Castle Peak Road near the junction with Chung On Street, Tsuen Wan, without prior notification to the FNO and without conducting cable detection or trial pits to locate the underground telecommunications line. The crew used a pneumatic breaker to remove paving and subsequently excavated with a mini-excavator within the clearly marked UTL corridor. A section of protective ducting was scraped and partially exposed, with no temporary support or barrier installed. The method of work did not include hand-digging when approaching the anticipated UTL alignment, nor were protective boards or warning tapes reinstated. These actions appear to contravene section 18A of the TO regarding the requirements for carrying out work near UTLs. I request investigation and appropriate enforcement action.
