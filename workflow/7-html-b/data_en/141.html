5. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-03-22 a HarborNet representative visited the subject party's office and promoted a "500Mbps Business Fibre" plan advertised at HK$198 per month with "free installation" and "no hidden fees". After sign-up, the welcome documents revealed a HK$68 monthly equipment rental and a HK$680 activation fee that were not mentioned by the salesperson. The first invoice also included a "staircase surcharge", and the connection was throttled after 300GB of usage despite no such limit being disclosed during the sale. Customer service refused to waive the undisclosed charges and advised that an early termination fee would apply if we cancelled. The complainant believes these omissions and misrepresentations contravene the fair trading provisions of the TDO.
