Response from the enforcement agency: Acknowledged report and referred for assessment by the Commercial Crime Bureau. Requested copy of the contract, call recording, calling number, and installation appointment records.

5. Please set out the details of the complaint (note 5):

On 2026-04-28 I received a telesales call from HarborNet Mobile offering a fibre broadband plan at HK$168 per month with "unlimited" data, free installation within 3 days, and a 12-month term. After giving verbal consent, I received an email contract dated 2026-04-28 showing a 36-month commitment, a HK$500 installation fee, and a Fair Usage Policy that throttles speeds after 200GB. Installation was rescheduled twice and no technician arrived on 2026-05-03. When I asked to cancel due to delays and undisclosed fees, I was told an early termination charge of HK$1,200 would apply. I believe the salesperson misrepresented key terms and omitted material information in breach of the fair trading sections of the TDO.
