Amlodipine Hypertension (blood pressure control) 10 mg Once daily (evening) tablet Take at the same time each day; may cause ankle swelling; do not abruptly discontinue without medical advice.
Sitagliptin Type 2 diabetes (glycemic control) 100 mg Once daily (morning with or without food) tablet Monitor blood glucose regularly; dose adjustment may be required in kidney disease; seek care for severe abdominal pain (possible pancreatitis).
Rosuvastatin Hyperlipidemia (lower LDL cholesterol) 20 mg Once daily (any time) tablet Report muscle pain or weakness promptly; avoid excessive alcohol; periodic liver function testing may be recommended.
NP Thyroid (desiccated thyroid) Hypothyroidism (thyroid hormone replacement) 60 mg Once daily (morning, 45 minutes before breakfast) tablet Take on an empty stomach; keep brand consistent; separate from calcium/iron supplements by at least 4 hours.

Levalbuterol HFA inhaler Bronchospasm relief (asthma rescue) 1-2 puffs per use As needed (every 6-8 hours PRN) inhaler Shake and prime before first use; use a spacer if advised; seek medical attention if rescue use becomes more frequent.

Pregabalin Neuropathic pain / fibromyalgia 75 mg Twice daily (morning and evening) capsule May cause dizziness or drowsiness; avoid alcohol; taper gradually when discontinuing as directed by your clinician.
