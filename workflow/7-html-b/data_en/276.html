Provide a reasonable description of each issue to be decided, specify the relief sought for each one and provide the facts and reasons for your objection (if you need more space, add a separate sheet).
Issue 1: Double-charged fuel volumes supplied to registered distributors and exempt end users. Relief sought: Reassessment to exclude volumes supplied under valid exemption certificates and volumes on which the counterparty remitted the fuel charge, with refund of overpaid amounts. Facts/reasons: During the assessment period, Prairie Logistics Fuel Co. delivered marked diesel and gasoline to farms and to registered distributors (registration numbers available). For those deliveries, exemption certificates were obtained (copies on file) and the counterparties are registrants who reported and remitted the charge. The assessment appears to have treated these sales as taxable retail sales by our company, resulting in double remittance. Shipping documents (bills of lading), customer exemption certificates, and sales summaries reconcile to 1,248,600 L that should be relieved or excluded. Issue 2: Incorrect provincial rate and period applied. Relief sought: Recalculation using the Saskatchewan fuel charge rates in effect for the applicable months and correcting for the rate change on April 1, 2026. Facts/reasons: The notice applied the Ontario rate to deliveries made exclusively within Saskatchewan from 2025-10-01 to 2026-03-31 and did not reflect the April 1, 2026 adjustment. Our delivery routes, tax zone coding in our dispatch system, and stamped invoices demonstrate the proper jurisdiction. Applying the correct SK rates reduces the assessed charge materially. Issue 3: Returned product and temperature correction not reflected. Relief sought: Reduction of assessed volumes by the quantity returned and allowance for standard temperature correction as per the Act and guidance. Facts/reasons: Several loads were returned or short-delivered due to quality holds and equipment failures. Credit notes CN-2026-0412 through CN-2026-0429 document 27,430 L returned in the period. Meter logs and automatic temperature compensation reports show corrected net volumes. The assessment appears to have used gross load tickets without applying these adjustments. Summary of requested remedies: exclude exempt and registrant-to-registrant volumes, apply Saskatchewan rates for the correct period, account for returned product and temperature corrections, and refund any overpayments with applicable interest. Supporting documents (bills of lading, exemption certificates, customer registrations, dispatch logs, meter and ATC reports) are available and attached on a separate sheet.
