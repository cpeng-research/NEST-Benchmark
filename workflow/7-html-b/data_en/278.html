Summary of circumstances:
1) Urgent relocation and compliance upgrades: On June 10, 2025, our primary drop-in centre received notice of a mandatory building closure for landlord-initiated fire code renovations. We were required to vacate by July 1, 2025 and relocate to a temporary facility. The relocation incurred unexpected non-program expenditures including commercial movers and transport ($7,500), fire code compliance upgrades at the temporary site (alarm panel, extinguishers, egress signage) ($4,000), and short-term storage for donated goods ($1,500). Insurance does not cover these items. These out-of-pocket costs total $13,000 and diverted funds previously allocated to qualifying program disbursements.

2) Program disruption: The move necessitated a three-week suspension of evening outreach and a two-week reduction in daytime services, resulting in lower qualifying disbursements for meals, hygiene supplies, and casework support. Based on historical averages and service records, the reduction in qualifying program disbursements is estimated at $11,000 for the fiscal year.

3) Reduced donations and fundraising revenue: The relocation coincided with our mid-year appeal and a local employer closure, producing an 18% shortfall in monetary donations relative to projections for May–September 2025. This represents an estimated revenue shortfall of $12,800, a portion of which would normally be directed to program disbursements during the fiscal year.

Net impact and reduction sought:
- Expected increase in non-program expenditures (relocation, compliance upgrades, storage): $13,000 (out-of-pocket) to be paid in the 2025 fiscal year.
- Estimated lost qualifying disbursements due to program suspension and reduced donations: $11,000.
- Total net shortfall against planned qualifying disbursements: approximately $24,000. After applying available reserve reallocation and minor expense cuts ($1,500), the charity requires a reduction of $22,500 to the Disbursement Quota for the 2025 fiscal year to avoid depleting operating reserves and maintain continuity of essential services.

Supporting documents attached (submitted with this application):
- Moving and compliance upgrade invoices and contractor estimates (dated Jun–Aug 2025).
- Lease termination notice, temporary facility agreement, and storage contracts.
- Monthly donation and fundraising reports May–September 2025 showing the shortfall.
- Calculation worksheet summarizing projected qualifying disbursements before and after relocation.

The requested reduction is temporary for the specified taxation year and is required to allow the charity to stabilize operations after relocation, restore program capacity, and maintain solvency while donations recover.
