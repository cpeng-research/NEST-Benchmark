I hereby object to the Notice of Assessment dated 2026/05/06 (Notice No. B9876543210456). Issue: disallowance of $2,950 in eligible moving expenses claimed for the 2023 tax year.

Reasons and relevant facts:
- In August 2023, I relocated from Calgary, AB to Vancouver, BC for a new employment position with a different employer. The move brought me more than 40 kilometres closer to my new workplace, meeting the eligibility criteria under the Income Tax Act.
- The claimed expenses include transportation and storage of household effects, travel costs (vehicle mileage and meals during the move), and lease cancellation fees. All amounts were incurred and paid in 2023 and were not reimbursed by my employer.
- Receipts, invoices, and a mileage log are attached. The T1-M calculation and supporting documents demonstrate that the amounts were reasonable and directly related to the move.
- The assessment appears to have treated the lease cancellation fee and a portion of travel expenses as personal; however, these costs are specifically permitted when directly linked to the eligible relocation.

Requested resolution: reassessment to allow the $2,950 in moving expenses and corresponding adjustment to taxable income, tax, interest, and penalties as applicable.
