Bluenose Marine Services Ltd. engages temporary deckhands and dock assistants on an as-needed basis during peak shipping periods. The ruling determined that certain casual workers were not dealing at arm's length with the payer and that all hours reported on availability logs counted as insurable. We are appealing because the workers were sourced through open calls, had no familial or ownership ties to the company, and negotiated their own acceptance of shifts. When individuals declined shifts, there were no consequences, and many worked concurrently for other marine operators. Only hours actually worked and recorded on signed timesheets should be considered insurable; standby/availability windows were not compensated and should not be included. We request that the arm's length determination be corrected and that insurable hours be recalculated to reflect timesheeted hours only for the stated period.
