For more information, see the back of this form. (Attach separate sheet(s) if necessary.)
During the assessed periods, the individuals performed services under written service agreements as independent contractors. They invoiced per milestone with HST, supplied their own equipment and software licences, carried liability insurance, and bore the risk of profit or loss. They set their own schedules, accepted or declined assignments, worked for other clients concurrently, and could engage assistants without prior approval. Company involvement was limited to specifying deliverables and deadlines. We believe the assessment improperly treated contractor billings as pensionable/insurable earnings and overstated CPP/EI by applying CPP2 before the additional threshold was met. Penalties and interest appear to include late-filing charges where remittances were made on time. We request a reassessment to recognize non-employee status and to correct CPP, CPP2 and EI calculations for the years in question.
