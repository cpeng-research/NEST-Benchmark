Please state the facts and reasons for your objection (if you need more room, add a separate sheet).
Summit Aero Components Corp. objects to the Notice of Assessment A-2026-1384 and Notice of Determination D-2026-0456 issued to Westbridge Industrial Traders Ltd. on the basis that tax was assessed on goods shipped to a designated foreign trade zone for immediate export and therefore properly zero-rated. The disputed total includes duplicate application of an environmental surcharge and misclassification of non-taxable airworthiness certification services billed on Invoice #SA-2026-118 (dated 2026-04-26). Supporting documentation includes the purchase order, export permits, carrier waybills, and correspondence confirming export treatment. The supplier has assigned to Summit Aero Components Corp. the right to object under a written assignment dated 2026-05-08. No portion of the disputed tax has been remitted; the tax component has been withheld pending resolution. We request reassessment to remove the ineligible tax, issuance of the related refund, and payment of statutory interest to the date of refund.
