Section IV – Circumstances and facts supporting your request
Information to support your request (attach an extra sheet if you need more space)
I unintentionally exceeded my FHSA contribution room for the 2024 tax year after opening a second FHSA at Prairie Credit Union while an existing $300 monthly pre-authorized deposit remained active at Summit Bank. This caused duplicate contributions in April and May 2024. I became aware of the issue on July 3, 2024 after reviewing the CRA contribution room update. I immediately contacted both institutions to cancel the duplicate deposit and requested a corrective withdrawal of the excess on July 8, 2024. The excess amount and any attributable earnings were removed on July 10, 2024. CRA later assessed monthly tax on the excess for May and June 2024 totalling approximately $185.00. A first review letter dated February 6, 2025 concluded that I did not act promptly. I respectfully disagree. Enclosed are: (1) confirmation of cancellation of the duplicate pre-authorized contribution dated July 4, 2024; (2) FHSA transaction history and bank statements; (3) a letter from Prairie Credit Union acknowledging an account setup oversight and processing delays; and (4) proof of the July 10, 2024 corrective withdrawal. These documents demonstrate that the excess was unintentional, that I acted in good faith and within a reasonable timeframe once aware of the issue, and that the situation has been fully rectified. I request a second review and reconsideration of the assessed tax.
