2. Explain the duties and responsibilities of management and key staff.
Sofia L. Andrade (President & Chief Executive Officer): Sets corporate strategy and long-range objectives, leads the executive team, oversees enterprise risk management, and serves as the primary liaison to the Board and major investors. Approves key capital allocations and partnerships, ensures a strong ethical culture, and represents the company with regulators, industry bodies, and strategic customers.

Marcus J. Patel (Finance Director): Oversees financial planning & analysis, budgeting, accounting, treasury, and tax. Implements internal controls, manages audits, optimizes working capital and capital structure, supervises procurement policies, and provides financial forecasts and scenario analyses to support strategic decisions and stakeholder reporting.

Yuko T. Sakamoto (Chief Product & Technology Officer): Directs product vision and technical strategy, manages R&D and engineering teams, and ensures platform reliability, cybersecurity, and data governance. Owns the product roadmap, architecture standards, release management, and the evaluation and integration of emerging technologies to drive innovation and scalability.

Harold G. Stein (Director of Operations): Manages day-to-day operations, fulfillment, supply chain, facilities, and vendor performance. Translates strategic plans into operational KPIs, drives process excellence and cost efficiency, ensures service level adherence, business continuity readiness, and continuous improvement across production and support functions.

Priya R. Menon (Chief Commercial Officer): Leads revenue strategy across sales, marketing, partnerships, and customer success. Develops go-to-market plans, pricing and packaging, pipeline development, brand and communications, and channel programs. Accountable for revenue targets, retention and expansion, and customer experience initiatives.

Gabriela C. Duarte (Director, People & Compliance): Leads talent acquisition, onboarding, performance management, compensation & benefits, and learning & development. Establishes compliance frameworks, policy management, and training programs; oversees employee relations, diversity & inclusion initiatives, and coordinates regulatory filings and internal compliance audits.

Collective responsibilities: The leadership team meets monthly to review performance against strategic and financial plans, prioritize cross-functional initiatives, manage enterprise risks, and approve material expenditures and senior hires. Executives coordinate on roadmap priorities, compliance obligations, financial controls, operational throughput, and customer outcomes, and provide regular reporting to the Board with action plans for continuous improvement.
