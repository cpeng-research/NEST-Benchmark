Summary of Content
A slide-based instructional program introducing core concepts of renewable energy, including solar, wind, hydro, and bioenergy. The set features black-and-white transparencies with detailed captions, data charts, and teacher prompts, designed for grades 9-12. Materials support STEM learning through case studies and classroom experiments, and include a large wall poster for group discussions. The program is silent and intended for guided classroom use.
