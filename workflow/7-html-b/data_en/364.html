Section 4 – Circumstances and facts supporting your request
I am requesting cancellation of the Part X.1 tax on excess RRSP/PRPP contributions for 2022 and 2023. In early 2022, my financial institution misapplied a direct transfer from a maturing group RRSP as a new contribution, and a retroactive employer PRPP match for 2021 posted in January 2022 was applied to my 2022 deduction room, creating an inadvertent excess. When I noticed the discrepancy after receiving my 2022 contribution receipt, I arranged for an immediate withdrawal of the excess and filed the required forms. In 2023, an automated pre-authorized contribution was duplicated due to a system error and was reversed within the same month once identified. The first review declined relief; I am submitting this second review with additional supporting documentation, including bank correspondence confirming the misapplication and reversals, contribution and withdrawal statements, and payroll records. I did not benefit from the excess amounts; any income attributable to the excess was removed along with the withdrawal. The assessed Part X.1 tax represents a significant hardship considering the errors were beyond my control and were corrected promptly. I respectfully request cancellation of the assessed Part X.1 tax based on reasonable cause and fairness considerations.
