PROCESS OVERVIEW
Process Description Purpose &Scope
This procedure establishes a standardized, risk-based approach to evaluate, qualify, and approve new suppliers. It includes intake of sourcing needs, pre-qualification screening, financial and compliance checks, quality audits, technical capability assessment, negotiation of commercial terms, ERP vendor creation, and cross-functional approval to add suppliers to the Approved Supplier List (ASL). Purpose: Ensure suppliers meet quality, compliance, ethical, and business requirements prior to onboarding. Scope: Applies to all new and reactivated suppliers providing direct materials, indirect goods, and critical services for Northstar Manufacturing Group. Involves Procurement, Quality, Finance, Legal, EHS/Sustainability, IT/ERP Administration, and Vendor Management teams.
Definitions &Related Documents
Definitions: 'ASL' - Approved Supplier List; 'PPAP' - Production Part Approval Process; 'ERP' - Enterprise Resource Planning system; 'NDA' - Non-Disclosure Agreement. Related Documents: Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire, Quality Audit Checklist, Code of Conduct, Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy, Sanctions Screening Procedure, Master Supply Agreement Template, NDA Template, Risk Assessment Matrix, ERP Vendor Creation Work Instruction.
PROCESS STEPS
WBS Task Owner
1.0 Receive supplier request or sourcing need and open qualification case Sourcing Specialist
1.1 Issue pre-qualification questionnaire and collect certifications, tax forms, and insurance Vendor Management Coordinator
1.2 Perform preliminary risk screening (commodity, geography, criticality) Risk Analyst
1.3 Conduct financial health review and complete sanctions/denied party screening Finance Analyst
1.4 Schedule and conduct quality audit (on-site or remote) per product risk Quality Auditor
1.5 Evaluate technical capability and review samples or PPAP submissions as required Quality Engineer
1.6 Complete compliance assessment (EHS, ethics, data security where applicable) Compliance Officer
1.7 Negotiate commercial terms and draft Master Supply Agreement and NDA Procurement Manager & Legal Counsel
1.8 Create supplier record in ERP and assign vendor number ERP Administrator
1.9 Compile qualification package for cross-functional approval and add to ASL Vendor Management Lead
2.0 Communicate approval decision and onboarding next steps to supplier Sourcing Specialist
2.1 Set ordering parameters (pricing, lead times, Incoterms, contacts) in ERP Buyer
2.2 Define quality requirements and incoming inspection plan Quality Manager
2.3 Enroll supplier in performance monitoring (OTD, PPM, nonconformances) Supplier Performance Analyst
2.4 Execute first article or initial order review and obtain sign-off Production Manager & Quality Engineer
2.5 Hold 30-day review to assess onboarding progress and resolve issues Vendor Management Lead
2.6 Conduct 60-day performance checkpoint and implement corrective actions if needed Buyer & Quality Manager
2.7 Conduct 90-day supplier review and confirm approved status or remediation plan Director of Procurement
