6. Detail the reasons for requesting EMT Selective Certification:
(Use additional sheets if necessary)
The city seeks selective certification to address critical vacancies among Firefighter/EMTs. A surge in multi-casualty incidents, scheduled deployment to new waterfront districts, and multiple long-term leaves have reduced daily unit availability. Several personnel are in training pipelines, temporarily decreasing the number of licensed EMTs available for field assignments. Selective certification will enable targeted hiring of candidates with current credentials to restore minimum staffing, maintain reliable ALS/BLS coverage on every shift, reduce overtime dependence, and ensure compliance with state response performance benchmarks while broader recruitment initiatives are underway.
