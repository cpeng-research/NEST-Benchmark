a. Identified Risk/Concern
Risk of falls due to declining to consistently use a walker and choosing to navigate stairs without assistance. Participant prefers to ambulate independently at home and in the community despite increased fall risk.
b. Details of the Participant's Plan to Address the Risk/Concern
Participant will keep pathways free of clutter, install two additional grab bars in the bathroom, and wear non-slip shoes at all times. Participant will complete daily balance and strength exercises (15 minutes) as instructed by home physical therapy and accept home PT twice weekly for six weeks. Participant will use the handrail on stairs and avoid carrying heavy loads; a laundry basket will remain on the ground floor. A medical alert pendant will be worn at all times. The Care Coordinator will call three times weekly for one month, then weekly for two months. If two consecutive contacts are missed, the emergency contact (daughter) will be notified and a wellness check requested. Participant will log any falls or near-falls and report dizziness or weakness to the SC RN Consultant or primary care provider within 24 hours. A home safety review will be completed quarterly.
c. Possible Consequences if the Risk/Concern is Not Addressed
Potential consequences include fractures (e.g., hip, wrist), head injury, internal bleeding (especially if on anticoagulants), emergency department visits, hospitalization, surgery, prolonged rehabilitation, loss of independence, long-term care placement, and death.
